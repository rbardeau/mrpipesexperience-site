Watch the Band Book the Band

Not an Impression. A Recreation.

Any singer can hit the notes. Mr. Pipes doesn't just hit them — he rebuilds the whole performance from the ground up: the phrasing, the growl, the breath control, the exact moment the original singer's voice cracks or soars.

One song it's Jim Morrison's baritone prowl. The next, Chris Cornell's four-octave wail, or Eddie Vedder's guttural rasp. Then Sinatra's silk-smooth phrasing, Hank Jr.'s outlaw twang, and Hendrix's psychedelic swagger, all in the same set.

Backed by a band that matches the era-correct tone for every legend, Mr. Pipes Experience isn't a tribute act to one artist — it's a living jukebox of rock and roll's greatest voices.
